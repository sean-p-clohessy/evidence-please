# Product specification

Evidence, Please currently gives leaders in General Further Education and Tertiary colleges short, useful rehearsal for professional challenge. Its college model includes education programmes for young people, adult learning, apprenticeships, high-needs provision, HE pathways and higher apprenticeships where within scope, and commercial or employer-responsive activity. The MVP supports Quick Fire, Written Practice and Mock Inspection sessions; six inspectors; contextual question filtering; typed self-review and prepared answer building; evidence selection; persona follow-ups; limited Push Me challenges; descriptive debriefs; and game-only rehearsal points.

The front-page Bodyswaps Interview Packs pilot contains two external-practice scenario families—Safeguarding assurance and Curriculum quality and coherence—combined with all six inspector styles. Each of the 12 generated packs contains a scenario, learner and AI roles, communication style, private memory prompts, an adaptive question bank, AI Coach questions, assessment criteria and recommended settings. Packs are copied manually into the separate Bodyswaps platform; Evidence, Please has no Bodyswaps account connection or data transfer.

It deliberately does not grade unrestricted writing, predict inspection activity, issue official judgements or store user information. Sessions exist in browser memory only.

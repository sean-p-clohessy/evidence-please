# Product specification

Evidence, Please gives leaders in schools, colleges, apprenticeship provision and other education settings short, useful rehearsal for professional challenge. The MVP supports Quick Fire, Written Practice and Mock Inspection sessions; six inspectors; contextual question filtering; typed self-review and prepared answer building; evidence selection; persona follow-ups; limited Push Me challenges; and descriptive debriefs.

It deliberately does not grade unrestricted writing, predict inspection activity, issue official judgements or store user information. Sessions exist in browser memory only.
